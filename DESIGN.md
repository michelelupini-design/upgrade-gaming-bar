# Existing arcade interface, prepared for sharing

Preserve the vivid blue/purple arcade atmosphere, glossy perspective buttons, UpGrade identity and readable glass panels requested by Michele. Remove vendor artwork and the Pandora logo from this distribution. Use the newly generated four-scene atlas, the existing original UpGrade lettering and original button/guide illustrations. Platform names use readable text until each third-party logo's distribution rights are established.

Keep navigation categories as stable internal identifiers so translation cannot alter filtering, favorites or saved state. Display text comes from keyed UTF-8 language files. Italian and English receive complete initial strings; Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Simplified Chinese and Japanese have supported locale slots and explicitly partial starter translations with English fallback. Fonts fall back to Android's system font for CJK text. This preparation does not claim a joystick-operated Japanese/Chinese IME or translation of game ROMs.

Do not introduce a timed intro. The original app launch background is replaced with our own window background; the device's preinstalled firmware boot animation is a distinct component and is not redistributed here.
